DocuHop: plan IDFA with your friends

DocuHop is a free, unofficial planner for IDFA, the International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam, from Thursday 12 to Sunday 22 November 2026. It is not affiliated with IDFA.

What you can do

  • See every public showing on one map, day by day, with halls, times and English subtitles.
  • Build your shot list: walking times between cinemas, clashes, and when to leave to be in your seat.
  • Plan with friends: a ticket board shows who has a ticket and who still wants one, and a grid finds the day everyone is free.
  • Buy tickets on IDFA's own site. DocuHop never sells tickets and doesn't know whether any are left.

Unofficial fan planner, not affiliated with IDFA. Times can change: always check the official page. Times from IDFA's public programme on idfa.nl.
